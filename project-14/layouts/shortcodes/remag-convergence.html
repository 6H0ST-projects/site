Defaults are a reference point, not proof of production or anisotropy accuracy.

Estimation method and source data

For each run: estimated total = measured total − measured cycle time + measured cycle time × (threshold step / final step). The cycle timers are upstream’s IterSCF and REMAG’s remag_device_cycle. Fixed work inside those timers is not separately resolved. The residuals were printed to six decimal places. No new default-threshold speedup is claimed.
