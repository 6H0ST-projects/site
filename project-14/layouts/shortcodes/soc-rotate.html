Interactive · schematic energy model

Model: E(θ) − E(0) = K sin²θ with K > 0 when SOC is on and K = 0 when it is off, in units of K. Real anisotropy energies are microelectronvolts to about a millielectronvolt per atom and are computed, not assumed. Shape (dipolar) anisotropy and higher-order terms are omitted.
